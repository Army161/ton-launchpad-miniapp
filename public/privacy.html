Privacy Policy

TON Launchpad collects minimal data to operate the Telegram Mini App:

  • Telegram profile — user ID, username, first name (via initData, validated server-side).
  • Wallet address — when you connect via TON Connect (linked to your Telegram ID).
  • On-chain data — token creations and trades are public on TON blockchain.

We do not sell your data. Session tokens expire after 7 days. Bot token and deploy keys are never stored in the app bundle.
